**Mile:** Section 7, Block 39, T-1-S, T&P RR Co. Survey (GLO Abstract 34), Midland County, Texas — now Green Tree North and Green Tree Country Club.

## 1. Verified narrative

### The Estes family ranch & farm (1911–1978)

The deed chain (source-log rows 11–21) shows the Estes family holding Section 7 for 67 years: S.W. Estes bought from the Holloways in 1911 → S.W. & Arminta Estes conveyed to Thelma Estes in 1921 → Ethel Aldredge Estes conveyed to Aldredge Estes Jr. in 1976 → Aldredge Estes Jr. et al. sold **surface only** to Midland West Corporation in 1978, with minerals severed and retained.

**"Button" Estes = Aldredge "Little Button" Estes, Jr.** — verified, not assumed. The obituary of his wife, Patricia Ann McCaskill Estes, states: "she met the love of her life, Aldredge 'Little Button' Estes, Jr. They were married March 1, 1947, in El Paso, Texas." ([Dignity Memorial](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/midland-tx/patricia-estes-6009699); also on [Legacy/GoSanAngelo](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/gosanangelo/name/patricia-estes-obituary?id=12792103))

The Estes operation was a real cattle-ranching enterprise, not passive landholding. Patricia Estes (born Dec 30, 1925, Sweetwater TX, to Malcolm Neubert McCaskill and Lucille Virginia Heatly; raised in Breckenridge, graduated Breckenridge High School 1945; worked at the Minute Inn Cafe and then Superior Oil Company as a well draftsman before marriage):

- "loved managing the family ranching business and raising quality **registered Hereford cattle**"
- active in 4-H, FFA, and the National Hereford Association
- helped in the establishment of the High Sky Girl's Ranch (later High Sky Children's Ranch)
- served on the Midland Memorial Hospital Board beginning 1979 — five elected terms, then the Board of Governors 1992 until her death
- member, First Christian Church of Midland
- died June 12, 2014; services June 16, 2014, Ellis Funeral Home, Midland; burial Fairview Cemetery, Midland
- children: Virginia Ann Estes and Aldredge Beau Estes III (wife Barbara Skaggs Estes); granddaughter Bailey Elizabeth Estes

**Button Estes Ranch, Ltd.** is a Texas domestic corporation incorporated 05/31/1996, address 2000 West Tennessee, Midland, TX 79701, holding 99 mineral interests across 5 counties ([breeder directory](https://creatures.com/directory/breeders/button-estes-ranch-ltd); [investor profile](https://www.elementix.ai/investors/button-estes-ranch-ltd)). This is most likely the family's vehicle for the minerals severed in 1978 — an inference, not proven.

**Ranch-name caution:** no historical source found names the Section 7 operation. Map language should say **"Estes family ranch/farm"** — NOT "Button Estes Ranch" (that name belongs to the modern mineral-holding entity and cannot be tied to Section 7 as a historical ranch name).

### Midland West Corporation and the Green Tree development (1977–1982)

The club's own history page is the primary source for the founding narrative ([greentreecc.com/about](https://www.greentreecc.com/about)):

- Summer 1977: **Jerry Mobley**, then head golf professional at Ranchland Hills Country Club, believed there was room for another club and shared the idea with **John Wood**.
- **Hoyle McCright** of First National Bank came forward with financing; **Midland West Corporation** was created.
- "320 acres north of the original site was obtained from Button Estes." (The Texas CMAA variant of the same history dates that acquisition to **1980**: [texascmaa.org PDF](https://www.texascmaa.org/files/Green%20tree%20CC%20Executive%20Chef%20Posting%20-%202025.pdf))
- Mobley recalled in a letter: "The site was a sandy old farm with one sickly tree and tumbleweeds."
- Unique design requirements caused friction: long cul-de-sacs, no alleys, no curbs. "We didn't even have a road, just a dirt trail to the property as late as February 1980."

The federal case record confirms the financing structure:

- *Midland West Corp. v. FDIC*, 911 F.2d 1141 (5th Cir. 1990) ([opinion PDF](https://static.case.law/f2d/911/case-pdfs/1141-01.pdf)): in 1982 Midland West borrowed **$8,946,311** from the First National Bank of Midland to develop the "Green Tree Property," plus a second $3.1 million note for unrelated oil-and-gas programs. **L. Frank Mullins** and **Edmund R. Rigatti** guaranteed portions of the debt; the bank secured the development note with five deeds of trust, including the Green Tree property.
- *United States v. McCright*, 821 F.2d 226 (5th Cir. 1987) ([Justia](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/821/226/255834/)): McCright was FNB's executive vice-president 1977–82, had a personal financial interest in Midland West, and was involved in a **$1.925 million FNB loan** for development of the Green Tree golf course and country club. He was convicted on false-entries counts relating to his FNB dealings.

Hoyle McCright biography ([Nalley-Pickle & Welch obituary](https://npwelch.com/obituary/william-hoyle-mccright-jr/)): William Hoyle McCright, Jr., born May 5, 1925, Dawson County; grew up on the family farm in the Roby area; Roby High School; U.S. Navy in WWII; Southwestern Banking School in Fort Worth; moved to Midland in 1948 and worked for First National Bank for his whole career; married Modelle Peel October 15, 1949; four children; longtime member of First Baptist Church of Midland; original member of the Midland College Board of Regents, involved in the location, planning, and building of Midland College; died October 19, 2014; buried Resthaven Memorial Park. (Note: the obituary says he "retired in 1980," while the federal case covers his FNB officer activity 1977–82 — flag the slight conflict rather than resolve it. One forum post claims he received a later presidential pardon; that is **not verified** against DOJ records.)

### Green Tree Country Club history

From the club's own history and corroborating sources:

- Architect: **Charles Howard** of Austin — original 18-hole championship course, ~176 acres, Bermuda fairways and bentgrass greens, "renown as the largest in the southwest at the time."
- **Opened ~1980–81.** Sources vary slightly: the club says "forty years ago" (page written ~2021); "Since 1980" ([ThreeBestRated](https://threebestrated.com/golf-courses-in-midland-tx)); "Opened: 1980" ([TexasGolf.com](https://texasgolf.com/golfcourses/green-tree-country-club)); "built in 1980" ([The Social Golfer](https://www.thesocialgolfer.com/golf-courses/united-states/texas/green-tree-country-club)); "first opened for play in 1981" ([ForeTee](https://foretee.com/courses/texas/midland/usa/green-tree-country-club/13953)). Print "opened ~1980–81."
- An **additional nine holes opened in 1985** → the 27-hole club that exists today (North, East, West courses).
- Later work: Tripp Davis & Associates became consulting architect and completed the three-hole "Road Course" short course and new practice facilities in **2018** (recorded pre-compaction at tdagolf.net).
- Club address: 4900 Green Tree Blvd, Midland, TX 79707-1509.

**Aerial reconciliation (important):** the 1986 USDA aerial shows the golf course under construction, which conflicts with an ~1980–81 opening — unless you remember the nine-hole expansion. The fresh-work look in 1986 most plausibly reflects the **1985 nine-hole expansion still maturing** (plus adjacent residential construction), not the original 18-hole build. The 1972 TXDOT aerial already shows graded corridors/cleared pads; 1995 shows the course mature and the subdivision substantially built out. Do not assert the 1986 imagery depicts initial construction.

### Green Tree North subdivision

- **Plat filed December 1, 1982** (instrument 1982-25784); developer/dedicator: **Midland West Corporation**, president **John Wood** (source-log row 4, clerk portal doc 39181134). Plat title block: "Being a part of Section ___, Block ___, T-1-S, T&P RR Co Survey."
- Index grantors on the plat include **HAILCO INC** and Midland West Corp (Hailco's role unresolved — see below).
- The Green Tree North restrictions and covenants ([gtn-hoa.org PDF](https://www.gtn-hoa.org/_files/ugd/6d46c4_daf0905b0f8b475a86d107466b2b6d14.pdf)) name Midland West Corporation as "Developer" and owner of the subdivision. A builder-pace clause is notable: original owners had to commence construction of an approved dwelling within **2 years** of acquiring title (30 months for subsequent owners), or the Developer retained a repurchase option at the original price — evidence the plan was rapid build-out from the start.
- Neighborhood character ([Homes.com profile](https://WWW.HOMES.COM/midland-tx/green-tree-neighborhood/)): custom contemporary builds from the 1980s — ranch, Tudor-inspired, and New Traditional styles, typically 2,000–5,000 sq ft, 3–5 bedrooms. (Secondary/weak source; consistent with the aerial sequence.)
- Two subdivisions exist: **Green Tree North** and **Green Tree South**, each with its own HOA-run events.

## 2. Could not confirm (margin candidates)

1. **Pre-1911 chain — RESOLVED 2026-09-26 (source-log row 39):** there is no "John D. Edwards" in the chain. The "DR/12/64" reference was a misreading of **DR/12/54**: Charles J. Canda → Mrs. Mary T. Edwards, deed recorded 1/6/1906 (doc 1906-1676), "T&P RY CO SUR-7 T-1-S BLK-39 640-ACS". The 1911 deed (DR/21/354, image read) recites the Book 12 p. 54 Canda deed; its "EDWARDS JOHN" is Mary's son/heir (wife Mamie), no middle initial. Related: Canda → West Edwards for Sec 17 (1899) and Sec 18 (1894/1905). The 1906 deed image itself was not viewable (portal outage) — retry later.
2. **Biographies of S.W. Estes, Arminta Estes, or Thelma/Ethel Aldredge Estes** — no reliable public-web biographies, obituaries, or census material located.
3. **Hailco Inc. — PARTIALLY RESOLVED 2026-09-26:** Midland homebuilder active by 1980, per *Macha v. Carameros* (Tex. App. 1984). Principals and exact role in the 1981–82 Green Tree transactions still unconfirmed. The "Hailco USA Corporation" found online filed in Texas on December 8, 1998 and was tax-forfeited in 2002 ([corporationwiki](https://www.corporationwiki.com/Texas/Hurst/hailco-usa-corporation/35419907.aspx)) — too late to be the 1981 entity; **do not use it**.
4. **First arm's-length lot sale, initial lot prices, first completed home** in Green Tree North — no dependable public source. Earliest homes appear in the 1986 aerial under construction; nothing print-safe before that.
5. **Contemporary newspaper coverage** — Midland Reporter-Telegram archives are paywalled; no accessible historical items found.
6. **Portal to Texas History, Handbook of Texas, THC Atlas** — no items located for these subjects via public web search; the Portal's internal search is not accessible from this environment.
7. **Jerry Mobley, John Wood** — no biographies beyond the club's founding narrative. **Charles Howard — PARTIALLY RESOLVED 2026-09-26:** Charles Howard, P.E. of Austin, designed Green Tree's original course (1980) plus Tomahawk/Comanche Trail (1990), Blackhawk, Champion Lakes. Middle name, firm, dates, obituary unconfirmed.
8. **Outcome of the FDIC/Midland West litigation** — the 1990 opinion documents the financing, not the resolution.
9. **Pre-railroad Indigenous history on the section itself** — no named trail, camp, spring, or event documented on Section 7; the Comanche War Trail passed ~35 mi NE via Big Spring and cannot be claimed for the mile. Midland Draw is the section's pre-railroad watercourse.
