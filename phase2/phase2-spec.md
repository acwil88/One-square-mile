## 1. Design decisions (made — not open)

**Sheet.** 24 × 36 in, portrait. Folds 3 across × 4 down to 8 × 9 in; fits a letter envelope. 1 in margin all round.

**Scale.** Main panel at 1:3,000 exactly — 1 inch = 250 ft. One mile = 21.12 in square. This is the largest a section fits on 24 in with margins, and 1:3,000 is a scale a surveyor would recognize.

**Vertical stack, top to bottom (all inches):**

| Zone | Height | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Top margin | 1.0 | — |
| Title block | 2.4 | Section name, edition line, the 1887 note |
| gap | 0.35 | |
| Main panel | 21.12 | The section, 1:3,000 |
| gap | 0.35 | |
| Aerial strip | 3.35 | Six frames, same crop, 1954 → 2022 |
| gap | 0.35 | |
| Text zone | 6.08 | Three columns: chain of title · the ground · could-not-confirm + colophon |
| Bottom margin | 1.0 | |

**Ink.** Three colors, that's all:
- Near-black `#1A1A1A` — everything.
- One blue `#4A6FA5` — water only (Midland Draw, wells).
- One red `#B3261E` — only for what was corrected or could not be confirmed. The 1887 red-ink correction is the reason. Red on this sheet means "the record disagreed with itself, or ran out."

Paper: print on white 28# or heavier bond (ask the shop for their heaviest plotter bond, not 20#). No printed background tint.

**Type.** Text: a serif with real small caps (EB Garamond or Source Serif 4 — both free). Labels on the map: a condensed sans (Barlow Condensed or IBM Plex Sans Condensed). Two families, nothing else.

**Main panel layers, bottom to top:**
1. Base: 2022 NAIP orthoimage, grayscale, lightened ~40%. Ground, not decoration.
2. 1982 plat lot lines — hairline gray. Street names in condensed sans, gray.
3. Golf course outline — hairline.
4. Soil unit boundaries — fine dotted; unit names in small caps (Amarillo, Midessa, Bippus).
5. Midland Draw — blue hairline, labeled.
6. 1978 conveyance parcel: SE/4, 160 ac, "surface only, 1978" — dashed black outline.
7. Wells: producing = filled circle; horizontal pad = filled square with a tick showing lateral direction; canceled = open circle with a slash. API numbers in 6pt condensed. City well and golf course well = triangle, blue.
8. Crude gathering line — dashed black, labeled "gathering line (T-4 10611, linkage unverified)."
9. The 1954/1966 topo pipeline — dashed **red**, labeled "PIPELINE, USGS 1966 — identity unconfirmed."
10. Quarter-section lines — dashed.
11. Section neatline — heavy black. **Along each edge, W.C. Powell's 1876 calls, in varas, in the survey's own wording.** This is the signature of the sheet.
12. Corners: tick + lat/lon to 4 decimals from the real polygon (not the eyeballed corners).
13. North arrow. Scale bar in feet *and* varas (1,900.8 varas to the mile).

**What is not on the sheet, by rule:** any owner name after 12/1/1982. Any house. Any resident. TWDB owner names. Phone numbers, addresses.

**Aerial strip.** Six frames, identical crop (section + 10% bleed), identical size (3.35 in square, 0.2 in gutters): 1954 · 1965 · 1974 · 1984 · 1995 · 2022. Caption under each: year, source, one line. 1937/1944 are not printed (watermarked / order-only) and are listed in the could-not-confirm column instead.

## 2. Margin copy (draft — Adam approves at proof stage)

### Title block

> **SECTION 7**
> Block 39, Township 1 South, Texas & Pacific Railway Company Survey · Abstract 34 · Midland County, Texas
>
> ONE SQUARE MILE — *Desk Edition.* Compiled from records, not walked.
> First edition, October 2026. One copy. Corrections invited; anyone who corrects it gets the second edition free.

Right side of title block, in red:

> Surveyed 1 February 1876 and docketed under **Martin County**. Corrected to Midland County in red ink, 1887, two years after Midland County was organized. The county this ground sits in was itself a correction.

### Column A — Chain of title, 1883–1982

> **1876.** Surveyed 1 Feb by deputy surveyor W.C. Powell, chain carriers L.E. Wright and E.C. Bennett, "on the waters of North Concho." Bearings in varas. Filed at the General Land Office 27 Dec.
> **1876.** Land Scrip No. 3123, 25 May: the Texas & Pacific has built 203 miles, 4,624 feet of railroad. This section is part of what that construction earned. Odd-numbered — railroad land, not school land.
> **1883.** Patented 15 Dec to Texas and Pacific Railway Co. Patent 540, Vol. 68, p. 503. 640 acres.
> **1905.** Charles J. Canda et al. → Mary T. Edwards. Deed Records 12/54. Three notes of $795.
> **1911.** Mary T. Edwards and heirs → S.H. Holloway, with Sections 6, 8, 17, 18. Buyer assumes the 1905 notes. Four months later Holloway → S.W. Estes. **The Estes years begin.**
> **1921.** S.W. and Arminta Estes → Thelma Estes, ~160 acres.
> **1976.** Ethel Aldredge Estes → Aldredge Estes Jr.
> **1978.** The Estes family → Midland West Corp., 31 Oct. Surface and surface rights only, SE/4, 160 acres. Minerals stay with the family. Consideration: ten dollars and the note.
> **1981–82.** Midland West → Hailco Inc. (plat-joinder parcel). BSD Inc. → Midland West, agreement.
> **1982.** Green Tree North plat recorded 1 Dec. Cabinet C, p. 134. Developer: Midland West Corporation.
>
> *The chain stops here. Sixty-seven years of one family; ninety-nine years of ranch. Everything since is somebody's home and is not this map's business.*

### Column B — The ground

> **Water.** Ogallala aquifer 100–180 ft below the section. One City of Midland well inside the line, 147 ft, unused. About ten domestic and irrigation wells drilled 2002–2021, 135–180 ft. Golf course well, 175 ft, 2021. Midland Draw crosses the north half and has carried its name on every USGS sheet from 1954 to 2019 — it never disappeared, the ranch did.
> **Soil.** About 60% Amarillo and Midessa fine sandy loam — well drained, "farmland of statewide importance." Bippus clay loam in the draws, subject to occasional flooding. No hydric soils. Water table below 80 in. A "sandy old farm with one sickly tree," as the man who sold it put it in 1977.
> **Oil.** Four producing wells and four horizontal pads on the north edge, laterals running south under the streets. A crude gathering line crosses the north half. Operators, dates, and depths could not be pulled (see margin).
> **Before the streets.** Open rangeland on every USGS edition 1954–1985: oil wells, drill holes, a gravel pit, a pipeline. Streets first appear 1991. Built out by 2010.
> **The course.** Summer 1977: two men buy 320 acres from the Estes family, one of them the head pro at Ranchland Hills. First National Bank finances it — $8.9 million, later litigated. Dirt-trail access until 1980. Course opens 1980–81; nine more holes 1985; rebuilt 2018.

### Column C — Could not be confirmed (set in red)

> Compiled from records, not walked. The following were looked for and not found, or found and not trusted:
> - Identity of the pipeline drawn on the 1954 and 1966 USGS sheets.
> - Whether the 1978 gathering line is the T-4 Permit 10611 "Green Tree" system. Likely; not shown by any single record.
> - Operators, completion dates, and depths of the eight wells (RRC identify service unavailable at time of compilation).
> - Which quarter Thelma Estes received in 1921.
> - Whether Thelma Estes and Ethel Aldredge Estes are the same person. No instrument says so.
> - Hailco Inc.: who they were.
> - The nature of the 1982 BSD Inc. agreement.
> - First lot sold, first house built, first price paid.
> - Any 1930s–40s aerial. A 1944 Air Force frame exists at TxGIO and was not ordered.
> - Whether "waters of North Concho" is hydrologically true. [pending NHD check — see §3]
> - Anything the Reporter-Telegram printed about this ground; the archive is paywalled.
>
> If you know any of these, write. Second edition is free to anyone who corrects the first.

### Colophon (small, bottom of Column C)

> Sources: Texas General Land Office (patent, field notes, scrip); Midland County Clerk (deed records as cited); Midland Central Appraisal District (abstracts, parcels); USGS topoView and EarthExplorer (topographic sheets 1954–2019; aerials 1954, 1965, 1974, 1984, 1995); USDA NAIP 2022; Railroad Commission of Texas GIS; Texas Water Development Board; USDA-NRCS Web Soil Survey; federal court records as cited. Full source log, thirty rows, available on request.
> Gathered by Skippy (Meta Muse). Designed by Claude (Anthropic). Approved and paid for by a resident of the section, who is not named on it. No phone calls were made.
