# BSD Inc. — 1982 Green Tree North purchase agreement (queue item 15)

**Instrument:** Doc 1982-719, DR/731/258–268 (11 pages). Recorded 2026-09-27 copy purchased from Midland County Clerk portal, $11.00, order #18976402. Local copy: `phase2/images/1982-719-bsd-agreement.pdf`.

## What it is (one sentence)

A January 8, 1982 Real Estate Purchase Agreement in which **Midland West Corporation sells a 20.343-acre tract (Lots 20–23, Block 6, the future Green Tree North) to THE GREENS, a joint venture of HAILCO, INC., DOVECOTE, INC., and BSD, INC., for $1,348,425 cash** — i.e., the developer-to-builder land deal that launched the Green Tree North subdivision on Section 7.

**Correction to the queue brief:** the clerk index lists this as "BSD INC → MIDLAND WEST CORP," but the agreement itself has Midland West as **Seller** and THE GREENS JV (Hailco + Dovecote + BSD) as **Buyer**. BSD Inc. is one of three joint venturers, not the counterparty.

## Property

- 20.343-acre tract out of Section 7, Block 39, T-1-S, T&P Ry. Co. Survey, Midland County (Exhibit A metes-and-bounds; Exhibit B plat shows Lots 20, 21, 22, 23, Block 6, fronting proposed North Midland Drive; ~75-ft TESCO easement on west line).
- §II describes "not less than 20.745 acres" but Exhibit A computes 20.343 acres; the exhibit controls.

## Key terms

- **Price:** $1,348,425.00 cash, paid in full as earnest money at execution, applied at closing (§III).
- **Build-or-reconvey (§VII):** Buyer must commence construction on each lot (per Architectural Control Committee plans) within 4 years of closing or re-convey the unbuilt lot(s) to Seller by warranty deed at the original price.
- **Seller's development obligations (§VIII):** annexation of the subdivision to the City of Midland; final platting; final zoning permitting single-family, duplexes, townhouses, and/or multi-family at ≤10 units/acre; extension of North Midland Drive paving; extension of water, electric, gas, and sewer; recorded restrictions/covenants; and ≥100 Green Tree Country Club membership units for Buyer and assignees.
- **Closing:** Lone Star Abstract Company, Midland; within 30 days after Seller completes §§V–VI, VIII, no later than 7/31/1982 without Buyer's written consent (§XII).
- **Security (§XV):** Seller posts a $1,348,425 irrevocable letter of credit from First National Bank of Midland, 1-year term, securing return of the purchase funds if Seller defaults.
- Buyer may freely assign the agreement without Seller's consent (§XIX).

## Why it matters to the mile

This is the paper moment Green Tree North goes from ranch section to subdivision: five days after execution it is of record, and within it Midland West commits to annex, plat, zone, pave, and service the land — while the builder JV (Hailco/Dovecote/BSD, the same Hailco that later builds the houses) takes the 20-acre starter tract with a 4-year build clock. The first homebuyer deed (Lot 18, Block 2, recorded 1/26/1983 — see `phase2/first-house.md`) follows almost exactly one year later.
