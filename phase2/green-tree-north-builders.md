# Who built Green Tree North? (research 2026-09-27)

**Question:** a handful of builders, or many?
**Verdict: MANY.** Green Tree North was built by a rotating cast of independent custom builders buying finished lots from the developer — not by one or two tract builders.

## The model: developer sold lots, builders built custom homes

- **Midland West Corp** (Frank Mullins) was the land developer. It sold finished lots to builders — it did not build the houses itself.
  - Proof: clerk deeds show MIDLAND WEST CORP → HAILCO INC lot sales (1980-15853, DR/692/692; 1981-12355, DR/712/543, GREEN TREE C lots). Reporter-Telegram 1983-04-11 feature describes Midland West as the developer (12 partners from 1979, Mullins majority Dec 1982).
- The houses are consistently described as custom, varied-style ("ranch, Tudor-inspired, New Traditional... far from cookie-cutter"), which fits a multi-builder custom market, not a single tract operation.

The Dec 1985 week: **7 new homes, 6 different builders** — and the article notes 3 of the 7 were in Green Tree Country Club Estates ($90k–$140k range; Teakwood Trace at $140,000 fits). The builder roster turned over completely between the Hailco era (1980-82) and 1985 — no single builder dominates across sources.

## Notes / caveats

- "Green Tree Country Club Estates" (permit article, 1985) maps to the Green Tree North plats — 4804 Teakwood Trce is listed under that subdivision name, and Hailco's 1980 deed of trust cites "GREEN TREE CC ESTATES." MCAD confirms the addition as "GREEN TREE NORTH" platted in numbered sections (SEC 1, 6, 7, 14, 21...).
- The Green Joint Venture (Murphy & Rochester, Midland-Odessa) had a separate 217-acre clustered-home/condo project in final platting in 1983 — townhomes/condos, not the 220 single-family lots.
- No source names a dominant builder. If anything, the evidence suggests the mix *widened* over time (Hailco early → half a dozen builders by 1985).

## What would nail it down

A clerk-portal (midland.tx.publicsearch.us) tally of grantors on first-sale warranty deeds in the GREEN TREE NORTH sections, ~1983-1990 — the builder is the grantor on each lot's first sale. That portal is JS-only and needs a live-browser session. The newspaper permit record corroborates but is a weekly snapshot, not a census.
