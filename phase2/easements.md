# Pipeline easements, Section 7 Block 39 T-1-S (queue item 17→13, 2026-09-27)

**Question:** who built the pipeline on the 1954/1966 topos, and who built the 1978-era gathering line?

## The 1954/1966 line: MAGNOLIA PIPE LINE COMPANY, 1940

- **Doc 1940-14694** — recorded 12/11/1940 (instrument 12/02/1940). Grantors: BROWN THELMA ESTES, BROWN W T → Grantee: **MAGNOLIA PIPE LINE COMPANY**. RIGHT OF WAY, DR/67/531 (2 pp). Index legal: "MULTIPLE TRACTS SEE INSTRUMENT"; preview text: ROW to "lay, repair, maintain, operate and remove pipe lines… for the transportation of oil and gas, and the products thereof, water, or any other fluid or substance" over a 640-acre tract in T&P RR Co., Blk 39, T1S.
- **Doc 1940-14671** — recorded 12/10/1940 (instrument 11/26/1940). Grantor: ESTES ALDREDGE → same grantee. RIGHT OF WAY, DR/67/511 (3 pp). Legal: SEC 6 BLK 39 T1S (adjacent section, same 1940 project).

Only pre-1954 pipeline ROW in the block. Consistent with the line labeled on both the 1954 and 1966 topos.

## The 1978-era gathering line: PIONEER NATURAL GAS COMPANY, 1979

- **Doc 1980-1076-1** — recorded 1/28/1980 (instrument 10/19/1979). Grantors: ESTES ALDREDGE JR et al → **PIONEER NATURAL GAS COMPANY**. RIGHT OF WAY, DR/673/712 (2 pp). Legal: T&P RR CO SURV, Block 39, Section 7, Township 1-S — Section 7 confirmed.
- **Doc 1980-1077-1** — recorded 1/28/1980 (instrument 10/08/1979). Grantor: MIDLAND WEST CORPORATION → same grantee. RIGHT OF WAY, DR/673/714 (2 pp). Same Section 7 legal.

Part of a larger multi-tract Pioneer project (consecutive docs 1980-1072-1 through 1980-1078-1, same day). Caveats: index names a natural-gas company rather than a crude gatherer, and instruments are dated 1979 not 1978; confirming commodity needs the image ($2/doc).
