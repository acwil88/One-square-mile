## Flags: people, animals, structures, weather, or sales on Section 7 / "the Estes place," 1911–1978

**Deed-chain context:** the Estes family held Section 7, Block 39, T-1-S from 1911 (S. W. Estes bought from the Holloways) until 1978 (surface-only sale to Midland West Corp). Most of the 27 hits concern a different Estes ranch near Monahans (Ward County) and are **not** flagged.

1. **1916-11-10 — POTENTIAL FLAG.** McKay Hartprove spent a Monday on "the Estes ranch north of town" helping vaccinate calves — a person and cattle on an Estes ranch north of Midland, squarely inside the S. W. Estes ownership window, but the item never names Section 7 and the family also had other Midland-area holdings.
2. **1921-07-22 — STRONGEST FLAG.** The annual Estes family reunion (58 of 85 members present) was held "out at the S. W. Estes ranch" — a dated gathering of people at the ranch of the Section 7 owner during his ownership, though again the item does not say Section 7 by name.

**Not flags (explicitly different places):** Aaron Estes ranch (Monahans, 1921), E. W. Estes ranch southwest of Monahans (1923–1949), H. P. Estes ranch near Monahans (1923), Aldredge Estes ranch at/near Monahans (1929–1932), Jack (L. M.) Estes ranch cornering Martin/Dawson/Gaines counties (1941). The 1939 and 1942 hits contain no ranch at all.

**Observation (not a flag):** the 1949-03-27 issue places a wedding at "300 West Estes Street" in Midland — an Estes-named city street, no land connection established.
