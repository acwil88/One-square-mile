# Thelma vs. Ethel — identity resolution (queue item 11)

**Question:** Was Thelma Estes (grantee on the 1921 deed, Sec 7 Blk 39 T-1-S) the same person as Ethel Aldredge Estes (grantor on the 1976 deeds)?

**Answer: No — they were two different women.**

- **Ethel Aldredge Estes** was Ethel Johnson, who married Aldredge Estes on 11/08/1919 (marriage license 1919-1, MR/2/238, recorded 11/14/1919). She was Aldredge Estes's wife, later his widow; the 1976 deeds (1976-13382/13383/13384, DR/615/436–438) are her distributing Block 39 interests to their children Aldredge Jr., Aldredge III, and Virginia Ann.
- **Thelma Estes** married a Brown and appears thereafter as **Thelma Estes Brown** (also "Brown Thelma Estes"), active in the records 1930–1980. She was kin to Aldredge (likely his sister) — both received conveyances from Arminta Estes and S.W. Estes — but not his wife.

**Decisive instrument:** the 10/31/1941 family partition — four consecutive instruments, DR/70/291–294:
- 1941-16692: POA, Jeffrey Iris → Estes Aldredge
- 1941-16693: POA, Brown Thelma Estes → Estes Aldredge
- 1941-16694: WD, Estes Aldredge [& Ethel] → Brown Thelma Estes, Sec 6 Blk 39 T1S (DR/70/293)
- 1941-16695: WD, Brown Thelma Estes → Estes Aldredge, Sec 7 Blk 39 T1S T&P RY CO SURV (DR/70/294)

Thelma and Aldredge conveyed to each other simultaneously — they cannot be the same person, and Thelma cannot be Aldredge's wife Ethel.
