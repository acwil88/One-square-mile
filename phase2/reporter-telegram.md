## "Green Tree" (golf course / subdivision)

- **1983-04-11, p. 5B (Metro edition)** — Feature by business-energy writer Leslie Haines, headlined "'Dry' conditions haven't stunted Green Tree," reports Green Tree South began in 1979 with the golf course opening July 1979, and that on March 1 the club members bought the clubhouse plus north and south courses from developer Midland West Corp for more than $6 million, while Green Tree North (nine more holes, 220 lots on 297 additional acres) was under construction with 85 lots pre-sold. → **Clipping:** `phase2/rt-1983-04-11-green-tree-5B.png` (full page).
- **1983-04-11, p. 5B** — Same feature notes developer Frank Mullins became majority owner of Midland West in December 1982 (the corporation began with about 12 partners in 1979 and was down to three), that all project financing was underwritten by First National Bank of Midland, and that the Green Joint Venture (Murphy and Rochester, a Midland-Odessa firm) had a 217-acre clustered-home/condominium development in final platting and zoning.
- **1982-11-21, p. 10D** — Classified ad offers a Green Tree Golf Course lot at $55,000, showing course-frontage lots trading at a premium by late 1982.
- **1982-11-21, p. 13D** — Real-estate ads list multiple Green Tree Country Club lots for sale plus a daily open house at 6213 Sequoia, evidencing a built-out, actively reselling subdivision.
- **1982-11-21, p. 12C** — A metes-and-bounds legal notice for the unrelated "349 Ranch Estates" (Section 12) uses the Green Tree Boulevard right-of-way line as a boundary landmark, confirming the boulevard was an established named thoroughfare by November 1982.
- **1982-01-07, p. 10A** — Realtor ad solicits "investment properties" in "Permian Estates, Fairway Park, and Green Tree," treating Green Tree as an established submarket.
- **1981-09-02, p. 1A** — Classified ad for a "Greentree CC Estates, course lot" confirms the golf course and adjoining estates lots existed by September 1981.
- **1977-12-11 and 1979-04-01** — Search-engine false positives (no "Green Tree" co-occurrence in the OCR text); no relevant hits.

## "Hailco" (homebuilder)

- **1981-11-25, p. 1A** — Front-page city council report notes a zone-change request from Neal Hail, president of Hailco Inc., to rezone lots bordered by Jackson, Carver, Nobles and Cuthbert streets to a mobile-home district (council deferred action to December 15).
- **1981-12-17, p. 2A** — Council approved the Hailco-backed rezoning of that Jackson/Carver tract to a mobile-home district on a 3–2 vote, with the Hailco president having stipulated homeowner lot purchase, paved sidewalks/driveways, and skirted units.
- **1982-01-27, p. 4A** — Zoning notices list requests "coming from Hailco Builders," including tracts at Fairgrounds Road/Pecan Avenue and Hemlock/Fairgrounds Road.
- **1981-03-01, p. 2A** — Building-permits roundup lists "Hailco Inc., 3408 Sycamore, $30,000," using the incorporated name.
- **1981-02-22, p. 4A** — Permits roundup shows Hailco pulling two $60,000 new-residential permits (4604 and 4606 Neely).
- **1980-12-14, p. 4A** — Permits roundup shows Hailco active across multiple addresses (4715/4717 Preston Drive at $60,000 each, 3818 Fredna Place at $55,000, 6208 Sequoia Ave. at $125,000, 3016 Fredna Place).
- **1980-08-03, permits section (page not legible in OCR)** — Permits roundup lists "Hailco Construction, 4200 Valley Drive, $60,000" and "4202 Valley Drive, $60,000."
- **1981-04-02 (both editions), p. 2A** — Hailco appears as a $50 donor in a charitable-contributions list, incidental but consistent with an established local business.

## "Ranchland Hills" (golf course)

- **1981-04-20, p. 2A** — Obituary of a Medina County rancher notes he co-founded and part-owned Ranchland Hills Country Club in Midland with H. L. Winkler in 1948, dating the area's earlier golf course a generation before Green Tree.
- **1977-08-24, 1979-01-07, 1979-01-15, 1979-01-20, 1980-03-04** — Storm-damage and social/banquet notices set at the club; incidental, no land-history content. (1981-05-19 was a search false positive.)

## Bottom line for the deed/land story

The newspaper record corroborates the chain: Midland West Corp (Frank Mullins, ~12 partners from 1979, Mullins majority owner from Dec 1982) developed Green Tree South from 1979 with the course opening July 1979, sold the clubhouse and both courses to the members for $6M+ on March 1, 1983, and was building Green Tree North (297 acres, 220 lots) in 1983 with First National Bank of Midland financing. Hailco Inc. (president Neal Hail) was a separate active Midland homebuilder 1980–1982, building single-family homes and developing a mobile-home district — no newspaper link ties Hailco to the Section 7 tract itself.
